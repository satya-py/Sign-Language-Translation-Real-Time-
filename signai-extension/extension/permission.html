Allow SignAI to use your camera

Asked once. Nothing leaves your computer.

Chrome cannot ask for the camera from a toolbar popup: the popup closes the moment the prompt appears, and Chrome then records that as “Permission dismissed”. That is why this page opened in a tab.

  1. Press Allow camera below.
  2. Chrome shows a prompt at the top of this tab — choose Allow.
  3. Go back to your call and press Start translating in the popup.

The camera has not been allowed yet.

If you pressed Block earlier

Open chrome://settings/content/camera, find this extension in the blocked list and remove it, then come back and press Allow camera.
