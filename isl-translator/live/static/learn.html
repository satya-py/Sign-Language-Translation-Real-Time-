Copy these signs in front of the camera

Every clip below is signed by a deaf signer from the INCLUDE dataset. Press a tile to play it on a loop, copy the movement, then drop your hands and wait about a second so the system knows the sign has finished. After two seconds of stillness the English sentence is built.

Sentences to try

Sign the words in this order, pausing between each one.

HELLO → HOW ARE YOUHello, how are you?
I → SICK → TODAYI am sick today.
I → HOT → NIGHTI have a fever at night.
I → WEAK → MORNINGI am weak in the morning.
I → COLD → NIGHTI am feeling cold at night.
I → MEDICINEI need medicine.
I → BATHROOMI am going to the bathroom.
MOTHER → SICK → YESTERDAYMy mother was sick yesterday.
PATIENT → BED → WEAKThe patient is in bed and weak.
THANK YOUThank you.

ISL has no separate sign for several clinical words, so the nearest sign is read in context: HOT = fever, COLD = chills, BAD = not feeling well.
